# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Item Vault mobile application (the "App") handles information. The App is designed to work offline and to keep your information on your device.

## 1. Information You Store

The App allows you to create and manage records such as products, warranties, receipts, documents, rentals, subscriptions, and other personal items. Information you choose to enter may include:

- Item titles, categories, tags, notes, and dates
- Custom text, date, number, choice, and password fields
- Photos and PDF attachments
- Warranty, expiry, reminder, and other record details

You decide what information to enter. The App does not require you to create an online account.

## 2. How Information Is Stored

Item records, settings, and attachments are stored locally on your device. The App does not upload your records to a developer-operated server or cloud database.

Password-type fields are stored using the device's secure storage facilities, such as Android Keystore or the iOS Secure Enclave/keychain where supported. Password values are protected and require device authentication before they can be revealed.

No security system can guarantee absolute protection. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and keeping your operating system up to date.

## 3. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions only when the related feature is used:

- **Camera:** to capture photos of receipts, documents, or other item evidence.
- **Photos or storage:** to select, save, or manage photos and files.
- **Notifications:** to show local reminders for dates and expiry events.
- **Biometric or device authentication:** to protect the App and reveal password-type fields.

You can manage permissions through your device settings. Refusing a permission may prevent the related feature from working, but it does not prevent you from using unrelated features.

## 4. Notifications

Reminder notifications are generated locally on your device. The App does not use a remote notification service to send your item data to the developer.

You can enable or disable notifications in the App settings or in your device settings.

## 5. Sharing, Backups, and Exports

The App does not automatically share your information. When you explicitly use a share, backup, restore, print, or PDF export feature, the selected information is passed to the operating system or application you choose.

You are responsible for reviewing the destination and privacy practices of any third-party app or service you choose to use for sharing, storage, email, messaging, printing, or backup.

Backups and exported files may contain personal information and attachments. Keep them private and delete copies you no longer need.

Password values are not included in standard backups. PDF exports may include item details and attachments; protected fields are masked unless you explicitly authenticate and choose an export flow that reveals them.

## 6. Data Collection and Tracking

The App does not intentionally collect or sell personal information. The App does not include advertising, behavioral tracking, or analytics operated by the developer.

The App may use open-source and platform libraries to provide features such as local storage, camera access, file selection, notifications, secure storage, image processing, sharing, and PDF generation. These libraries are used to provide the App's functionality and are not intended to transmit your item records to the developer.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your information remains on your device until you edit or delete it, uninstall the App, or remove the relevant application data. Deleting the App or clearing its data may permanently remove locally stored records and attachments unless you have created a backup.

Backups, exported PDFs, shared files, and copies stored outside the App are not automatically deleted when you delete an item or uninstall the App. You must remove those copies from their destination separately.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose utility and is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated service. Because the App stores data locally, parents or guardians should supervise its use where appropriate.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published with the App and will include a revised effective date.
