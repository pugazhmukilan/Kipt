## What is Kipt?

Kipt (also known as **Item Vault**) is a personal organizer app for the things in your life that come with **paperwork, dates, and secrets**.

Think about how much mental load you carry every day:

- *When does my laptop warranty end?*
- *Where is the bill for the fridge I bought two years ago?*
- *When is my car insurance renewal? Is my gym membership about to expire?*
- *What was the password for that rental account? Who do I contact for the return?*
- *Can I find last year's electricity bill when I actually need it?*

Right now, the answers live in a messy drawer, a shoebox of receipts, a dozen screenshots, and your memory. **Kipt collects all of it into one private, searchable vault that lives entirely on your phone.**

It is built around a simple, powerful idea: **you create an "item" for anything — a product, a document, a subscription, a rental — and then attach exactly the information that matters for it**, in whatever shape you need. Nothing is forced into a rigid form the app designers imagined. You decide what a record looks like.

## The Problem Kipt Solves

1. **Paper fades.** Thermal printer receipts become unreadable within a year. Kipt lets you photograph them once, forever crisp.
2. **Dates slip.** Warranties, insurance, subscriptions, and Memberships all expire silently. Kipt tracks every date and warns you before it's too late.
3. **Information scatters.** The bill is in an email, the serial number is on a sticker, the login is in one of five password notes. Kipt gives every item **one page** with all of it together.
4. **Passwords get lost.** Rental portals, warranty registrations, locker combinations, Wi-Fi details — Kipt stores credentials in your phone's secure hardware, revealed only with your fingerprint or face.
5. **Phones get lost.** One-tap backup bundles everything (records + photos) into a single file you can restore on any device.

## Core Features

### 1. Flexible Items with Custom Fields

Every record in Kipt is an **Item** — a title plus *whatever fields you want*:

- **Text fields** — serial numbers, warranty periods, contact names, policy numbers, amounts, anything.
- **Date fields** — purchase date, expiry date, renewal date, return date. Any date you need to remember.
- **Password fields** — protected credentials stored in your device's secure hardware (Android Keystore / iOS Secure Enclave). Revealed only with biometric authentication, auto-hidden again after a short time, and auto-cleared from the clipboard after you copy them.

You aren't constrained to a fixed template. Track a TV with warranty + bill photo, or track a bank locker with a combination password + payment receipt — both look exactly as they should.

### 2. Date Reminders That Actually Work

Attach reminders to any date field:

- Pick how much advance warning you want — **7, 30, 60, or 90 days** before the date (or use the app default).
- Get local notifications on your phone — no account, no server, no spam. The reminder fires whether or not you have internet.
- The **dashboard** shows live counters: how many things are *Active*, *Expiring Soon*, and already *Expired* — so you can see your entire risk at a glance.

### 3. Photo & PDF Evidence, Built In

- **In-app camera** — photograph bills, receipts, ID cards, serial plates, or property condition, with the framing you need.
- **Gallery import** — attach existing photos straight from your phone.
- **PDF attachments** — attach statement PDFs, e-invoices, or policy documents directly to an item.
- A clean **attachment grid** on every item keeps all the evidence one tap away.
- Save any photo back to your phone's gallery whenever you need it.

### 4. Smart Bill Scanner

Photograph a bill and Kipt's **bill reader (OCR)** pulls out the important details for you automatically:

- Seller name and buyer name
- Invoice number and bill date
- Currency, subtotal, tax (GST/VAT), and grand total
- Payment method (cash / card / UPI, etc.)
- GSTIN / tax ID, phone numbers, and email addresses

Review, correct if needed, and save — turning a paper bill into structured, searchable data in seconds.

### 5. Login Credential Safe

For items that come with accounts — rental portals, insurance sites, warranty registrations, gate access apps — Kipt offers **login blocks**:

- A **named credential group** (e.g. *"ICICI Bank credentials"*) with a username field and a password field.
- Passwords live in **secure hardware storage**, never in the plain database.
- Viewing a password requires **fingerprint / Face ID / PIN**, every time.
- Copying a password auto-clears the clipboard after ~45 seconds so it doesn't linger.
- Shown passwords re-mask automatically after ~20 seconds if you walk away.

### 6. Beautiful PDF Reports

Generate a polished, branded **PDF report** for any item — with all fields, tags, notes, and a photo gallery laid out professionally:

- Perfect for **warranty claims** (attach the report to your claim email).
- Ideal for **insurance claims** after damage or theft.
- Great for **reselling** — hand a buyer a professional document with proof of purchase and conditions.
- Photos render in a clean grid, and even attached PDF documents get converted into the export, so the report is **one complete, self-contained file**.

### 7. Biometric App Lock

- Lock the entire app behind **fingerprint / Face ID / device PIN**.
- Auto re-lock whenever you background the app — someone picking up your unlocked phone still can't get in.
- Your data stays protected even when the phone is in someone else's hands.

### 8. One-Tap Backup & Restore

- **Export** everything — items, fields, categories, tags, and all photos and PDFs — into a single backup file you can keep anywhere (Google Drive, your PC, a pen drive).
- **Restore** on a new phone in one tap. Losing or upgrading your phone no longer means losing your paper trail.
- Password fields are deliberately **excluded** from backups for safety — they stay locked inside the device's secure hardware (just re-enter them once after a restore).

### 9. Organization That Scales

- **Categories** — built-ins like General, Personal, Finance, Health, Documents, Vehicle, Home, Tech — or create your own, rename them, recolor them, reorganize them.
- **Tags** — free-form labels (e.g. `#kitchen`, `#tax-documents`, `#2026`) filterable across everything.
- **Favorites** — pin the items you use most to the top.
- **Instant search** — find any item by title, tag, category, or content.
- **Sort & layout control** — sort by name, date added, or category; choose your grid density.

### 10. Private by Design

Kipt is **offline-first**. Your records, photos, and passwords live on your device. There is:

- No sign-up, no account, no email required
- No cloud server receiving your data
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking
- No internet permission requirement for daily use

You are the only person who can see your data. That's the point.

## How to Use Kipt — A Practical Walkthrough

### First Launch

1. Open Kipt — a short welcome screen introduces the app.
2. Set up your preferences from **Settings**: enable **notifications** (so reminders can reach you), pick a **light or dark theme**, and turn on the **app lock** if you want biometric protection.
3. Optionally give categories a look under *Settings → Manage Categories* and adjust them to fit your life.

### Everyday Flow

**Add your first item:**

1. Tap the **+** button.
2. Give it a **title** — e.g. *"Samsung Refrigerator"*, *"Car Insurance — Maruti Swift"*, *"Apartment Rent Agreement"*.
3. Pick a **category** and add **tags** if you like.
4. Add **fields** — for a fridge that might be:
   - `Serial number` (Text)
   - `Purchase date` (Date)
   - `Warranty expiry` (Date, reminder ON, 30 days lead)
   - `Service center phone` (Text)
5. Tap the camera to **attach a photo of the bill**. Done — the paper receipt can now leave your wallet forever.

**Stay ahead of deadlines:**

- The **home dashboard** shows your counters: 12 Active, 2 Expiring Soon, 1 Expired. Tap into the warning items and act.
- When a reminder notification arrives — *"Warranty expiry for Samsung Refrigerator is due in 7 days"* — you have time to schedule a service or register an extended warranty. Not after it lapses.

**When a date passes:**

-_expired items stay visible so you know what has lapsed — handy for claiming what's still claimable, or deciding a replacement._

**Claim that warranty:**

- Open the item → tap the **PDF export** → a complete, professional report is generated with all details and bill photos.
- Share it directly to email/WhatsApp/the service center's portal. Your claim documents took 10 seconds, not an hour of drawer archaeology.

**Never lose a login again:**

- On any item, add a **login block** (e.g. *"Property portal login"*).
- Store the username plainly and the password securely. When you need it, tap to reveal with your fingerprint — copy it, and it clears itself from the clipboard shortly after.

**Photograph a complex bill:**

- Instead of typing, use the **bill scanner** on your item — Kipt reads the seller, invoice number, date, amounts, tax, and payment method right off the photo. Fix anything it misread and save.

**Protect it all:**

- Once a month (or after adding important stuff), open *Settings → Export Backup* and keep the file somewhere safe. On a new phone, *Import Backup* brings back everything — records, dates, and photos.

## Tips for Getting the Most Out of Kipt

1. **Photograph the bill the day you buy it.** Old thermal receipts fade in months — your saved photo won't.
2. **Turn reminders ON for every date that has a consequence** — expiries, renewals, return windows, policy dates. Pick 30 days lead for most things, 7 days for commitments.
3. **Use tags as your personal filing system** (`#warranty`, `#insurance`, `#tax`, `#home`) — categories for big buckets, tags for questions you'll ask later.
4. **Favorite the two or three items you'd panic without** — insurance, passports, main agreements.
5. **Back up after every batch of additions, not once a year.** A backup made after data loss is worth nothing.
6. **Fill in the small details while they're fresh** — serial numbers, service center numbers, renewal contact names. Future-you will be grateful.
